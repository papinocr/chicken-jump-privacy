Chicken Jump - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Chicken Jump mobile game.

1. Information Stored by the Game

Chicken Jump does not require you to create an account or provide your name, address, or phone number to play. The game may store your score or game progress locally on your device using local storage.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

Chicken Jump may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and advertising-related data to provide, measure, and improve advertisements, subject to applicable settings and consent.

Advertising technologies and data processing are subject to Google's policies and the consent choices available in the app.

Learn more about how Google uses information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

You can review Google's Privacy Policy here: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by law, users may be asked to make choices about advertising and the use of personal data. Available choices depend on the user's location and the consent tools presented in the app. You can review or change available choices through the app's consent options, where provided.

4. Data Retention and Security

Game data stored locally remains on your device until it is removed by the game, cleared through your device settings, or otherwise deleted. Data processed by Google AdMob is handled according to Google's policies and retention practices.

5. Children's Privacy

Chicken Jump is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and data practices must comply with applicable child-protection and privacy laws. If you believe information has been handled improperly, please contact us using the details below.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, delete, correct, or restrict the processing of personal data. For data processed by Google, you may also need to use Google's privacy tools or contact Google directly.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
